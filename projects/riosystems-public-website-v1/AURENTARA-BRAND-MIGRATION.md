## C) Visual direction

Target feeling:
- calm
- precise
- trustworthy
- premium
- international
- technologically strong
- controlled

Formula:
- 70% trust + clarity
- 20% premium + authority
- 10% controlled wow

Base visual language retained from the existing website:
- near-black / graphite surfaces
- titanium / silver typography and borders
- restrained System Blue + Violet activation
- strong negative space
- Swiss-grotesk typography direction
- architectural system visualization
- CSS/SVG-first motion and rendering

Wordmark direction:
`AURENTARA SYSTEMS`
- uppercase
- wide but disciplined tracking
- no decorative futuristic display face
- calm Swiss-grotesk / neo-grotesk direction
- AURENTARA carries primary visual weight; SYSTEMS remains integral but may be optically quieter in a future custom lockup
